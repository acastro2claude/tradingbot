Zone Replay

Each clip replays the 10 minutes before price tests one of your zones, with the order book heatmap, the tape, delta and absorption, and then up to 5 more minutes. Trade it like you would live: press Long here or Short here at the moment you would enter, or Skip. The date is hidden and nothing after the clip is shown.

What's on the screen
  • Heatmap: resting orders per price, brighter = bigger compared with the day's typical size. Bid below price, ask above.
  • White line: last price. Bubbles: all the volume traded every 2 seconds, at its average price, like Bookmap's volume dots. Bigger = more contracts. Green = buyers outweighed sellers (buyer delta), red = sellers outweighed buyers; the stronger the imbalance, the stronger the colour, and grey = about even.
  • Diamonds: absorption bursts like your add-on (50+ ES or 100+ MES contracts at one price within 0.01 s). Purple = aggressive buyers absorbed by sellers (bearish). Yellow = aggressive sellers absorbed by buyers (bullish; green on your add-on). The size is written next to the bigger ones.
  • Shaded band: the zone being tested (its timeframe and test number are on the right). Bottom panel: delta every 10 seconds and cumulative delta.
  • The small chart on the right: 15-minute candles of the last sessions up to the start of the clip, with the zone.
  • Your entry is recorded at the time and price shown when you press. Results are scored later with your risk structure; you won't see them here.
Your decisions can't reach the shared store from this view, so they stay in this browser only. Open the page signed in to claude.ai to save them for Claude.
