ES Zone Review

Every zone the bot marks, on the five timeframes you trade. Scroll or pinch to zoom, drag to pan, double-click to reset. Click a zone on the chart or in the list, then mark it Valid or Noise and say why. Your marks save for both of us.

How the bot marks zones right now
  • Basing candle: the candle right before the move away. Red before a rally, green before a drop. A doji (open and close almost the same) also counts.
  • Move away: one candle at least 1.5× the basing candle's size (high to low) and 1.5× the last 20 candles' average, closing beyond the basing candle.
  • Price must move away 1.5× the zone's height before it first comes back. If it comes straight back, it was chop and there is no zone.
  • Drawn your way: from the basing candle's body (its open) to the wick. For supply, the upper wick of the basing or drop candle; for demand, the low wick of the basing or rally candle.
  • Tests are numbered. A new test needs price to move away by the zone's height (at least 5 points) first. From the 4th test the circle is faint: probably on its way out.
  • A zone ends when a candle on its timeframe closes beyond the far edge, when price sits inside it for 4 candles of its timeframe, or after 31 days.
  • No overlaps: the higher timeframe wins. A lower-timeframe zone that a higher one later formed over is not shown.
  • Candles start at midnight Chicago time, like thinkorswim. Prices are in the contract shown above the chart.
